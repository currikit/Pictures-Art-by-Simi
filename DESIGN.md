# Pictures Art by Simi: design record

## Direction (approved by the client, Nov 2026)
Modern creative studio. Clean pastel mockup is the visual reference.
Personality: creative, friendly, emotional, playful, youthful, personalised, handmade with love.

Rules:
- Every section has breathing room. No scrapbook clutter: few stickers, few borders, soft shadows only on cards.
- Large whitespace, clear section separation by space and soft background bands.
- Consistent horizontal alignment on one 1200px column.
- Soft pastel backgrounds, rounded product cards, clean product photography.
- Strong hierarchy: big bold headings, one pink italic accent phrase in the hero.
- Subtle playful accents only: a drawn heart, a sparkle, a slight tilt on hero photos.

## System
- Font: Poppins 400/500/600/700 and 700 italic. Logo script: Pacifico.
- Colour:
  - Pink #D6336C for buttons and large type (white on it 4.6:1).
  - Deep pink #B8245A for pink text and links (5+:1 on every pastel).
  - Ink #2B1B3D for text. Muted #6E6378 for secondary text (4.7:1 on the pastels).
  - Page #FFFAFC.
  - Pastels: pink #FCE4EE, blush #FFF0F5, lavender #EFE9FC, mint #E6F5EC, peach #FFEDE2, sky #E6F1FC, lemon #FFF5D9. Each product category has one pastel.
- Shape: cards 22px radius, images 14 to 16px, buttons and chips are pills.
- Motion: one easing, cubic-bezier(.25,1,.5,1). Hover lifts of 2 to 6px, 200 to 260ms. One idle motion: a hero photo floats gently. Everything is still under reduced motion.
- Logo: camera mark with a heart, "Pictures Art" in script, "BY SIMI" pink tag.

## Decisions that differ from the mockup
- Real photos from Simi's Instagram replace the mockup's generated product images and people.
- No cart or wishlist icons: there is no checkout. Ordering is on WhatsApp. Search is real.
- "Easy WhatsApp orders" replaces "Secure & Easy Orders" until online payment exists.
- The hero is one composition, not a carousel.
